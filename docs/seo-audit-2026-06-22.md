## ⏳ Deferred (content work — recommend follow-up)

- **10 thin articles (<1,000 words)** vs the 3,399-word median — all pre-existing (earliest posts): `ai-agents-driving-browsers-ndjson`, `ollama-free-local-stack`, `exploratory-testing-ai-agent`, `markdown-tests-living-documentation`, `openrouter-hundreds-of-models`, `selenium-page-objects-vs-plain-english`, `secrets-masking-credential-safety`, `ci-exit-codes-no-parsing`, `cross-grid-one-flag`, `smoke-tests-in-plain-english`. Expand each to ~2,000+ words.
- **~53 raw article titles still >60 chars** even without the suffix — need manual frontmatter tightening (or an optional `seoTitle` field). The suffix fix handled the bulk; these are the long-by-themselves remainder.
- **Near-duplicate "alternatives" trios** worth consolidating/canonicalizing: `browse-sh-alternative(s)(-2026)` (3), `kane-cli-alternatives(-2026)` (2), several `*-computer-use-alternative(s)` pages. Pick one canonical per tool or differentiate by sub-intent.
- **In-body contextual cross-links** — add 2–4 sibling links per article body (the token-aware related block helps, but in-body links pass more weight). Best ROI: the RPA / computer-use / scraping clusters.
